Every prediction is backed by a day-by-day seniority simulation, not a rough estimate. Click “Show simulation log” on any forecast result to see the full run — here is what it means.

1

Day 0 — Normalize

The simulation starts today. It checks each grade's sanctioned seats against how many are actually filled, removes anyone who has already retired, and immediately promotes the most senior eligible people upward to fill any open vacancies.

2

Forward Simulation — Day by Day

From there, the simulation jumps forward to each future retirement date. Every time someone retires, their seat opens up and the next most senior person is promoted into it — cascading through BS-19 → BS-20, BS-18 → BS-19, and BS-17 → BS-18 as needed. The tracked person's rank is recalculated after every step.

3

Highlighted Milestones

Whenever the person you searched for is promoted — or retires before a promotion comes through — it is called out in a highlighted box like this, so the key moments are easy to spot in the log:

TARGET PROMOTED BS-19 → BS-20
4

Final Result

The log ends with a summary: the person's name, retirement date, and the projected date(s) they reach BS-19 and BS-20 — or “Not Reached” if retirement or the simulation horizon comes first.

This transparency means you are never just trusting a predicted date — you can scroll through the exact seniority math and promotion cascade that produced it.

ⓘ
About This Tool

The Promotion Predictor estimates an employee's promotion due date based on Seniority Lists. It can be updated with fresh Seniority Lists, increased SNEs, and revisions to the Four-Tier Ratio.

Dynamic factors such as deferred cases and future vacancies are not included in prediction. The predicted date is indicative only; actual promotion is subject to Board 1, Board 2.
